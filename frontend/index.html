Social Media Privacy Risk Assessment Framework

Answer 44 short questions about your privacy settings and habits. You get a 0-100 privacy risk score (higher = more exposure), category scores, findings, prioritised recommendations, an improvement simulator and a printable report.

Start Privacy Assessment   View Dashboard

Privacy-first by design

  • No scraping, no tracking, no real-profile lookups.
  • You only answer YES/NO style questions - never type phone numbers, e-mails or addresses.
  • Only scores and finding types are stored; your raw answers are not.

Important

This is an educational risk framework based on self-reported answers. It is not a guarantee that an account will or will not be compromised. Weights and thresholds are educational assumptions.
