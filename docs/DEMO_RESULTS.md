## Recommendations
- [IMMEDIATE] Limit phone-number visibility where possible (friends only or hidden).
- [IMMEDIATE] Avoid publicly broadcasting real-time location unless intentionally needed; share with trusted people only.
- [IMMEDIATE] Enable multi-factor authentication using the strongest supported method (authenticator app or security key).
- [IMMEDIATE] Use a unique password for each account; change reused passwords starting with e-mail and social media.
- [IMMEDIATE] Never share verification codes; no genuine support team will ask for them.
- [IMPORTANT] Hide the birth year (or the whole date) from the public.
- [IMPORTANT] Share travel photos after returning, and keep trip dates private.
- [IMPORTANT] Verify unfamiliar profiles before accepting connections (mutual friends, profile age, behaviour).
